What is Cymatics?

Cymatics is a way of making vibration visible through physical media such as water, sand or a vibrating surface. The pattern is a physical demonstration of vibration. Any spiritual or consciousness-based meaning in this journey is offered as personal exploration, not as a scientific claim.

Health & Safety

Keep sound at a comfortable level. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, headache, distress or unusual discomfort. Do not use intense sound close to the ears. This tracker is for wellbeing and experiential exploration, not medical treatment.
